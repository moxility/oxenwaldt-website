Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → TOGAF 10 Practice → Cancel. Apple processes the cancellation; you keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in to the app with the same account email, then go to Settings → Restore purchases.

The AI Tutor isn't working on my iPhone

The on-device Apple AI tutor requires iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence enabled (Settings → Apple Intelligence). On older iPhones the tutor shows a friendly "unavailable" message — Practice, Mock Exams, and the full question library work on every device.

How do I delete my account?

Email magnus@oxenwaldt.com from the email address linked to your account. We delete within 30 days.

The app crashed

If a crash happens with Sentry diagnostics enabled (default), we get the stack trace automatically. If you can also email a short description of what you were doing, that helps us reproduce.

I found a question with a wrong answer

Email the question (a screenshot is great) to magnus@oxenwaldt.com. We update the content database server-side, so corrections appear in everyone's app on their next pull-to-refresh — no app update needed.

How often does new content land?

Server-side updates ship continuously. New questions and topic improvements appear on pull-to-refresh — typically several times per week.

Can I get a refund?

Subscription refunds are processed by Apple per Apple's policies. Request via reportaproblem.apple.com. We can advocate on your behalf if you email us.
