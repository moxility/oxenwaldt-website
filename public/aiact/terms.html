What the App is — and what it is not

The App is a preparation and documentation tool. It guides you through capturing the AI systems your organisation uses, derives a suggested risk classification for each from an encoded methodology, maps obligations to the EU AI Act’s application timeline, and generates registry and action-plan documents.

The App does not provide legal advice. Every classification it produces is a recommendation with its reasoning shown — it may be wrong, incomplete, or outdated, and it only becomes part of your registry when a person in your organisation reviews and accepts it. Regulation changes and every organisation’s situation differs. Have your assessment validated by qualified legal counsel before relying on it. You remain solely responsible for your organisation’s compliance with the EU AI Act and all other laws.
