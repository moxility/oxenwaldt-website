Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. If you delete the app, your assessment is gone — export the registry regularly to keep a copy.

Why does a system show “To Be Determined”?

Because you answered that its AI status is unknown. That is deliberate: an honest unknown beats a confident wrong answer. Confirm with the system’s owner whether AI features are enabled, then edit the facts — the classification updates instantly.

Why is voice prefill unavailable?

AI-assisted prefill uses Apple Intelligence on-device and needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence enabled. The interview works fully by typing and tapping without it.

How current is the legal timeline?

The methodology data ships with a content version (shown on the Overview and Timeline screens) and reflects the EU AI Act as amended by the Digital Omnibus. When the law moves, the data is updated in an app update — check the App Store for the latest version.

Managing your subscription

Navigator Pro is managed by Apple: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device. See the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
