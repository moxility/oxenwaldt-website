Azure AI Exam Prep — Support

Need help? Email magnus@oxenwaldt.com, or use "Report an issue" in the App's Settings tab. I read everything personally and reply within 1 business day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Azure AI Exam Prep → Cancel. Apple processes the cancellation; you keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Settings tab in the App → "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I delete my account?

Email magnus@oxenwaldt.com from the email address linked to your account. We delete within 30 days.

I found a wrong or unclear question

Use "Report an issue" in the App (category: Content error), or email a screenshot to magnus@oxenwaldt.com. We update the content database server-side, so corrections appear in everyone's app without an app update.

The AI Tutor says it is unavailable

The tutor runs on Apple Foundation Models and requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence enabled (iOS 18.1+). On older devices the rest of the App works normally.
