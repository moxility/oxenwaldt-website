Privacy Policy

Azure AI Exam Prep · Last updated 2026-07-19

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS app Azure AI Exam Prep (the "App"), developed by Magnus Oxenwaldt ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and shares information.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a random user ID, stored with our backend provider Supabase (EU region).
  • Study data: your practice sessions, answers, scores, streaks, and per-domain progress — used to power your stats and weakness analysis.
  • Subscription state: whether you have an active Pro subscription, processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash reports (Sentry) and anonymous product analytics such as screen views and button taps (PostHog), not linked to your identity.

The AI Tutor is on-device

The AI Tutor runs on Apple Foundation Models on your iPhone (iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence). Your tutor questions are processed on your device and are not sent to our servers or to any third-party AI provider.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or ad tracking.
  • We do not share your practice data with anyone, including Microsoft.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, email magnus@oxenwaldt.com from the email address linked to your account. We delete within 30 days.
