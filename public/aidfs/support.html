Common questions

Why do I need Apple Intelligence for the on-device Advisor?

The Advisor runs on Apple Foundation Models, which ship with Apple Intelligence. You need iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings. If your device isn’t eligible, the Advisor will let you know.

How do I keep my progress across devices?

Add your email in Settings → Account → Sign in with email. Your progress will follow that account instead of the anonymous session on your device.

How do I export or delete my data?

Email magnus@oxenwaldt.com with the email address you signed up with. I’ll return a JSON export or confirm deletion within 30 days.

My AI Advisor says “offline” — what do I do?

The on-device Advisor needs Apple Intelligence, which requires: iPhone 15 Pro or newer, iOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings → Apple Intelligence. If the check keeps failing after that, email me the error message shown and I’ll help.
