Privacy Policy

AI Don’t Fix Stupidity · Last updated 2026-07-20

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS app AI Don’t Fix Stupidity (the “App”), developed by Magnus Oxenwaldt (“I”, “me”), collects, uses, and shares information.

What I collect

Account information (optional)

On first launch, the App signs you in anonymously via Supabase. No email, no password, no personal details are required to use the App.

During onboarding, the App optionally asks for:

  • Email address — if you provide it, I use it to identify your account across devices and to contact you about the App.
  • Name, company, role, country — if you provide these, they help me understand who the App is reaching. All fields are skippable.

App usage

The App stores your interaction to power features:

  • Progress — which chapters you’ve opened and finished, your XP, your streak, your assessment score.
  • Plan checkmarks — which items you’ve marked complete on the ninety-day plan.
  • Runtime preferences — which AI Advisor tier you’ve chosen.

This lives on your device and syncs to your anonymous account row in Supabase, so your progress survives reinstalls when you’ve provided an email.

Diagnostics

If the App crashes or hits a runtime error, I collect a crash report via Sentry. The report includes the stack trace, device model, and OS version. Crash reports do not include your email or profile fields.

Product analytics

The App uses PostHog to understand which screens are visited and which features are used, so I can improve the App. PostHog events are linked to a randomly-generated app-install identifier, not to your email. You can opt out by deleting the App.

AI Advisor

The Advisor runs entirely on your iPhone via Apple Foundation Models (iPhone 15 Pro and newer with Apple Intelligence enabled). Your Advisor conversations never leave your device. I do not collect, store, or have any access to anything you type into the Advisor.

What I do NOT do

  • I do not sell your data to third parties.
  • I do not show third-party advertising.
  • I do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • I do not share your progress or profile with anyone.

Data storage

Account and progress data are stored in Supabase (Postgres) in the EU (Frankfurt region). Crash reports are stored in Sentry. Product analytics are stored in PostHog (US region).

Your rights

You can:

  • Access all data I hold about you by emailing magnus@oxenwaldt.com.
  • Correct any profile field by contacting me.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by emailing me — I will action within 30 days.
  • Export your progress and profile by emailing me — I will provide a JSON export within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy materially, I will update the “Last updated” date at the top and notify active users in-app on next launch.
