Common questions

“On-device writing is unavailable”

BoardMemo uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models, which need an iPhone 15 Pro or later running iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri). If the model is still downloading, the app will say so — try again in a few minutes.

Dictation doesn’t start, or stops early

Allow both Microphone and Speech Recognition for BoardMemo in Settings › BoardMemo. Dictation has no short time limit — you can talk for as long as you need. If your chosen language isn’t supported for on-device dictation on your device, add it under Settings › General › Keyboard › Dictation Languages, or type your notes instead.

Where are my memos stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, so memos cannot be recovered if you delete the app. Use the mail or share button to keep a copy elsewhere.

How do I email a memo to myself?

Set your address under Settings › Email memos to, then tap the mail icon on any memo. It opens a pre-filled draft in Apple Mail — you choose whether to send.

Can I make my own memo format?

Yes — Settings › My templates (or “My templates” on the Compose screen). Give it a name and your own section headings; it then appears alongside the built-in formats.

Managing your subscription

BoardMemo Pro renews monthly. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your device. To move Pro to a new device, use Restore purchases in BoardMemo’s Settings with the same Apple ID.
