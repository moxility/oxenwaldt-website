Privacy Policy

BoardMemo · Last updated 2026-07-25

BoardMemo is built on a single principle: your notes never leave your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. BoardMemo has no account, no login, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, store, or have any access to:

  • the notes you type or dictate,
  • the memos BoardMemo writes,
  • your voice or any audio recording,
  • your templates, settings, or the email address you set,
  • analytics, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, or crash telemetry.

How BoardMemo works

  • Writing. Memos are generated by Apple’s on-device Foundation Models, which run entirely on your iPhone. Your notes are never sent to us or to any third-party AI service.
  • Dictation. Speech is transcribed using Apple’s on-device speech recognition. The audio and the transcript stay on your device.
  • Storage. Your memos, templates, and settings are stored locally using the operating system’s standard app storage. Deleting the app deletes them permanently. We hold no copy and cannot recover them.
  • Email. When you tap the mail button, BoardMemo opens a draft in Apple’s Mail app with the memo already filled in. You choose whether to send it. BoardMemo does not send email itself and has no mail server.
  • Offline. BoardMemo works in airplane mode. It needs no network connection to write memos.

Subscriptions

BoardMemo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple’s App Store. Payment is handled by Apple and charged to your Apple ID.

Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, Inc., which processes an anonymous, randomly generated identifier and your purchase receipt solely to determine whether your subscription is active. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any memo content. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

No memo content, notes, or audio is ever shared with RevenueCat, Apple, or anyone else. Your free-tier usage count is stored only on your device.

Permissions

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — used only while you are actively dictating, to transcribe your speech on device. Nothing is recorded to a file or uploaded.
  • Photo Library — BoardMemo does not access your photos. iOS requires this declaration because the mail component is capable of attaching images.

Children

BoardMemo is a professional writing tool intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13.
