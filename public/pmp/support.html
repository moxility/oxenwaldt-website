Common questions

What's in the app right now?

339 questions across 27 topics, mapped to the PMP Examination Content Outline: eight topics on the People domain, thirteen on Process, four on Business Environment, plus a topic on agile and hybrid frameworks and one on the formulas. Every question carries a written explanation that also says why the wrong answers are wrong. The library grows server-side, so new questions appear without an app update.

Is this app affiliated with PMI?

No. It is an independent study aid. Every question is written from scratch against the publicly available Examination Content Outline; no PMI text is reproduced. Book the exam and read the official outline at pmi.org.

How long are the mock exams?

The Full Mock Exam is 180 questions in 230 minutes — the same shape as the real sitting. The Mini Mock Exam is 60 questions in 77 minutes, which is the same pace at a third of the length. Both draw on knowledge and situational questions together, as the real paper does.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → PMP Exam Practice → Cancel. Apple processes the cancellation; you keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Sign in to the app with the same account email, then go to Settings → Restore purchases.

The AI Tutor isn't working on my iPhone

The on-device tutor requires iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence enabled (Settings → Apple Intelligence). On other iPhones the Tutor shows an "unavailable" message — this is expected. Practice and the full question library work on every supported device.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and permanent, and removes your practice history and progress along with your login. Note that it does not cancel an active subscription — only Apple can do that, in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

The app crashed

Crashes are reported automatically via Sentry diagnostics. A short email describing what you were doing helps us reproduce it faster. See the Privacy Policy for what diagnostics include — during early launch this also covers session replay.

I found a question with a wrong answer

Email the question (a screenshot is ideal) to magnus@oxenwaldt.com. Content lives in a server-side database, so corrections reach everyone on their next refresh — no app update needed.

Can I get a refund?

Subscription refunds are processed by Apple per Apple's policies. Request one via reportaproblem.apple.com. We can advocate on your behalf if you email us.
