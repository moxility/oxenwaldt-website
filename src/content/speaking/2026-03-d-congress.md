D-Congress is the largest retail and e-commerce event in the Nordics, drawing thousands of attendees from across Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland to Gothenburg every March. I joined Stage F3 on Day 2 (March 5, 09:00–10:10) for a session organized in partnership with Svensk Handel, AI Sweden, and Columbus, exploring how artificial intelligence is reshaping retail.

## What we covered

The session focused on the practical reality of agentic AI in retail — moving past the demos and into the operational changes that actually move metrics. Topics included:

- How AI is changing the customer experience layer (search, recommendations, conversational commerce)
- The shift in how product information and pricing decisions get made
- Logistics and supply chain decisioning under AI assistance
- Responsible and transparent AI as a competitive lever, not just a compliance burden
- Lessons from Nordic retailers (Axel Johnson, Ellos Group, Apoteket, Systembolaget, Bubbleroom) moving from pilots to production

## Co-presenters

- Josephine Darlington (AI Sweden)
- Leaders from Axel Johnson, Ellos Group, Apoteket, Systembolaget

The Nordic retail community is unusually pragmatic about AI. The conversation wasn't about "is this real?" — it was about "how do we govern this responsibly while still moving fast enough to matter."
