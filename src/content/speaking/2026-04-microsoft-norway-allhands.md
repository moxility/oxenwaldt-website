I had the privilege of standing on stage at **Microsoft Norway's all-hands in Oslo** — not as an attendee, but as a speaker. Columbus was invited to share how we're bringing Agentic AI to life for our customers.

## What we presented

Our **Frontier approach** to Agentic AI: how Columbus is building, deploying, and governing autonomous AI agents inside enterprise systems — Dynamics 365 customer engagement, finance and operations, and the broader Microsoft stack. The session ran alongside **Gjert Hammersland** and **Didrik Nettelhorst Krog**.

## Why it mattered

When Microsoft asks a partner to come inspire their internal team on AI, the conversation becomes less about "what does your platform do" and more about "what are customers actually doing with this, and how do we close the gap between capability and value." That's the conversation we wanted to have.

A big thank you to **Helge Johansen**, **Rune L. Johannessen**, and **Kay-Arne S. Aarlie** for the invitation and for making us feel welcome. This is what partnership looks like — not just implementing each other's technology, but genuinely learning from each other.
