The best AI leaders I know share one trait.

It's not technical skills.
It's not an MBA.
It's not industry experience.

It's curiosity.

They ask "what if" more than "why not."
They experiment before they strategize.
They learn by doing, not by reading reports.

In my podcast conversation about AI leadership, we uncovered something fascinating:

The leaders driving real AI value aren't the most technical.

They're the most curious.

They:
- Try tools before evaluating them
- Ask "stupid" questions that lead to breakthroughs
- Challenge assumptions with experiments, not opinions
- Create safe spaces for teams to fail fast

Curiosity + experimentation > Strategy + analysis
