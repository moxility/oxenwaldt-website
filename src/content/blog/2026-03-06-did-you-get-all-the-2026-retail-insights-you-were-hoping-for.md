Did you get all the 2026 retail insights you were hoping for at D-Congress? 💭
If not, we’ve gathered a few of the most important ones for you.

Our experts Garret Cunningham, Magnus Oxenwaldt, and Dan Andersson share their event reflections in the video.  🎬 Here are a few of the key takeaways highlighted in their summaries.

- Strong ecosystems matter: Partnerships are becoming just as critical as the technology itself in the playfield of AI and agentic commerce

- AI is moving from hype to real use cases: Especially in product discovery and customer experience

- Winning brands connect discovery with storytelling: Your product and brand must work together to capture attention and loyalty

- Time-to-value beats theoretical ROI: The retailers moving fastest focus on rapid experimentation and real business outcomes rather than waiting for perfection

- AI belongs in the now, not the roadmap: The leaders are iterating today to unlock hyper-personalised experiences, operational efficiency, and new growth models

So, don’t wait for the perfect AI strategy - stay curious, start building, and act now!
