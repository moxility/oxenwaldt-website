Your digital colleague just got promoted.

Last year: It answered questions.
This year: It takes action.

The evolution is real:

2023: "Here's the report you asked for"
2024: "I noticed an anomaly and drafted a summary"
2025: "I found the issue, fixed it, and updated the team"

We're moving from digital assistants to digital colleagues.

Colleagues that:
- Join your meetings and take action items
- Monitor your dashboards and flag issues
- Draft communications you actually send
- Handle routine decisions within guardrails

On Future Bytes, I explored what this means for how we work.

The answer surprised me: The biggest change isn't the AI.

It's learning to delegate to a machine.
