How Elon’s war with OpenAI became Anthropic’s biggest data center.

Your AI provider went down again this week, didn’t it?

Look at the chart — Anthropic over the last 30 days, pulled directly from its own status pages. Partial disruptions nearly every week. If you’re building anything serious on top of these models, your team’s internal status page probably looks the same.

Mine does.

I mapped my own workdays using the same format. Roughly 89% of the productivity variance correlated directly with upstream lab status.

This is the silent tax distorting agentic systems in production:
retry logic, backoff strategies, circuit breakers, orchestration complexity, and engineering hours spent compensating for instability instead of building product value.

Today, that changed.

Musk owns xAI — a direct competitor to Anthropic. He has spent years fighting OpenAI. And now Colossus 1 reportedly shifts into Anthropic’s orbit:
300+ MW,
220,000 NVIDIA GPUs,
coming online within weeks.

Either compute scarcity now overrides ideology and competitive instinct, or Elon hates OpenAI more than he competes with Anthropic.

Possibly both.

But the market signal is the same.

▸ Claude Code 5-hour limits doubled
▸ Peak-hour throttling removed for Pro and Max

Architectures previously dismissed as too compute-heavy are back on the table. Parallel agent orchestration starts becoming the default. Retry scaffolding starts becoming deletable code.

Compute is the moat now.

Not models.
Not UX.
Not features.

The next 18 months will separate the labs that secured capacity years in advance from the ones still negotiating for it.

⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯

and locked down before competitors fully understood the constraint.

Reliability is a feature.

About time it started shipping like one.
