Can we trust AI?

Wrong question.

Better question: Under what conditions SHOULD we trust AI?

Trust isn't binary. It's a spectrum.

I'd trust AI to:
- Process invoices at 2am
- Flag anomalies in 10M data points
- Draft a first version of a report

I wouldn't trust AI to:
- Make the final hiring decision
- Set company strategy alone
- Handle a crisis without human oversight

The companies getting AI right aren't blindly trusting or blindly fearing.

They're building trust frameworks:
1. Define where AI decides autonomously
2. Define where AI recommends, humans decide
3. Define where humans lead, AI supports
4. Monitor, learn, adjust boundaries
